Common questions

Is agri.in free for farmers?
Yes — search, prices, weather, schemes, helplines and contacting businesses are completely free. We never charge farmers, and we never add commission to any price.
Does agri.in sell seeds, tractors or produce?
No. We list every option near you — businesses, brands, services — and you deal with them directly. We list, you choose, we never sell and never touch the money.
Are the mandi prices and scheme details reliable?
Mandi prices come from Agmarknet and are stamped with market and time. Every scheme page shows the official source it was verified against and the date it was last checked by our team.
What does the Verified badge mean?
Our team has checked the business's documents before the badge is shown. Verification can never be bought — and paid placements are always marked "Sponsored".
What are AgriCoins and can I cash them out?
AgriCoins are loyalty rewards you earn across agri.in, milk.in and theorganic.in — one balance everywhere. They unlock perks on the platform but are never cash and never transferable; the full rules are in the Coins center.
Who can see my phone number?
Nobody, until you choose. Contact reveals are consent-first and logged (DPDP Act 2023). You can export or delete your data anytime from your profile — and we never sell data to anyone.
